## Normative Language

**MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are requirements. Technology choices explicitly marked `PROPOSED` are not binding until accepted.

---

Mentora enables reviewed course publication, personal/group teaching, grounded Q&A, controlled voice turns, captions/accessibility, governance, audit, and recovery. Success metrics include review seconds per slide, grounded-answer acceptance, session/resume success, accessibility defects, incident rate, and cost per student hour.

The product excludes autonomous lecturer replacement, official high-impact decision making, open-mic group surveillance, and guaranteed perfect arbitrary-source understanding.
